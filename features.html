Features

Everything the app does to stop misinformation.

Detailed features for instant truth scoring, screenshot detection, regional language support, and community-informed trust.

Instant truth score

Shows a percentage score for the input text, making it easy to decide whether a message is suspicious.

Screenshot & forward detection

Detects screenshot cues and forwarded message patterns common in WhatsApp chains.

Regional languages

Supports English, Hindi, and Gujarati, so the app works on the most widely shared regional rumors.

Forward risk meter

Shows HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW risk so users know whether they should share or stop.

Viral spread simulation

Predicts how fast a message can spread if forwarded, making the risk emotional and memorable.

Community trust score

Uses votes to calculate a trust score, so repeated content becomes easier to evaluate over time.

Build focus

Keep the app simple, fast, and practical.

The best winning product is one you can use immediately. We keep the experience clear, avoid unnecessary complexity, and focus on what makes forwarded misinformation dangerous.
