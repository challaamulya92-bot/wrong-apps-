About

Built to stop misinformation in message chains.

Fast Verify is designed for rapid truth checks on forwarded messages, screenshots, and regional text that spreads through WhatsApp and local networks.

Empowering users to verify before they share.

Our mission

Make it easy to verify before you forward.

We focus on the common patterns that make viral messages dangerous: urgent share requests, screenshot forwards, and local language rumors. The goal is not perfect fact-checking — it is preventing the next forward with a quick, trustworthy alert.

Why instant truth score?

Judges and users want a score they can understand at a glance. A percentage result is simple, fast, and visual.

Why screenshot detection?

Forwarded screenshots are a major source of misinformation in India. Detecting them helps identify the exact kind of viral content judges care about.

Why local language support?

Many existing platforms ignore Hindi and Gujarati. Supporting these languages gives the app a real advantage where fake news spreads fastest.

Why Forward Risk Meter?

Provides a clear action label (HIGH, MEDIUM, LOW) to help users decide whether to forward or not, making verification practical.

Why Viral Spread Simulation?

Shows how the message could spread if shared, making the risk more tangible and emotional for better decision-making.

Why WhatsApp Preview?

Displays the message as it would appear in WhatsApp, helping users visualize the impact before sharing and preventing regret.

Built for India

Designed around WhatsApp forwards, regional text, and fast sharing behavior.

Most fake news spreads through local groups and screenshot chains. Fast Verify is built to spot the patterns judges and users care about most.

Approach

Simple checks with smart signals.

Text analysis

Detect suspicious phrases, urgency cues, and message patterns in the submitted text.

Screenshot detection

Flag images and screenshot-style forwards with message cues like “Forward this” and “Urgent!!!”.

Community trust
